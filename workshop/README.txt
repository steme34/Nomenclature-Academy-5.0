NOMENCLATURE GAMEPLAY DRAFT v4

Changes:
- Each Workshop station now records completion after a full round.
- Completed stations show a green check badge on their station card.
- Completion checks persist in localStorage across reloads for evaluation.
- Reset Draft XP also clears the draft station-completion checks.
- Typed-answer stations automatically focus the answer field when a new compound loads.
- After Compound Control routing unlocks a typed answer, its field is focused automatically.
- Students can therefore type → Enter → feedback/advance → immediately type the next answer without clicking the field again.

Prior v3 behavior retained:
- automatic next-compound progression
- Enter submission/fast advance
- fixed vs variable charge follow-ups
- spellcheck/Grammarly opt-out attributes
- chemistry subscript preview
